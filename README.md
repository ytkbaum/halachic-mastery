## 🌟 Vision

Halachah is vast. Mastering it takes years — often decades — of dedicated study. Yet most learners navigate this landscape without a map, without a guide, and without a system built around *them*.

The **Halachic Mastery System** changes that.

We are building an intelligent, adaptive platform that:

- **Meets you where you are** — assessing your current knowledge across the full halachic landscape
- **Builds your personalized roadmap** — based on your background, goals, and learning style
- **Tests and adapts dynamically** — using cutting-edge educational technology that responds to your progress in real time
- **Weights what matters** — leveraging a carefully constructed map of halachah that reflects the relative frequency, importance, and interconnectedness of each area

This is not just another learning app. It is a mastery system — designed from the ground up to produce genuine, lasting command of halachah.

## ⚙️ How It Works

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   HALACHIC MASTERY SYSTEM                   │
│                                                             │
│  1. INTAKE          2. MAPPING          3. ROADMAP          │
│  ──────────         ────────────        ────────────        │
│  Background    →    Your position   →   Custom path         │
│  assessment         on the full         through the         │
│  & goals            halachic map        halachic map        │
│                                                             │
│  4. LEARNING        5. TESTING          6. ADAPTATION       │
│  ──────────         ────────────        ────────────        │
│  Structured    →    Dynamic        →    System adjusts      │
│  content            assessment          in real time        │
│  delivery           engine              to your progress    │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Step 1 — Intake & Assessment
The system begins with a sophisticated onboarding assessment that maps your existing knowledge across halachic domains. This is not a simple quiz — it is a calibrated diagnostic that identifies both what you know and how solidly you know it.

### Step 2 — Personalized Roadmap Generation
Using your intake results alongside a weighted map of all of halachah, the system generates a custom learning roadmap. Priorities are determined by your goals (e.g., practical daily halachah, rabbinic preparation, comprehensive mastery) and the structural weight of each topic.

### Step 3 — Adaptive Learning & Testing
As you learn, the system continuously tests and re-tests. Content is spaced using proven retention science. The difficulty and focus of assessments shift based on your performance — ensuring you are always challenged at exactly the right level.

### Step 4 — Dynamic Adaptation
No two learners follow the same path. The system watches your progress, identifies gaps, surfaces areas of weakness, and re-routes your roadmap accordingly — automatically.

## 🗺️ The Halachah Map

At the core of this system is a comprehensive, weighted map of halachah — one of the most ambitious components of this project. This map:

- **Covers all major areas** of practical and theoretical halachah
- **Assigns weights** to each topic based on practical frequency, breadth of application, and foundational importance
- **Models relationships** between topics — so the system understands that mastering X is a prerequisite for Y
- **Is continuously refined** as we develop and receive community input

### Sample Domain Structure

```
Halachah
├── Tefillah & Brachot          [Weight: High — daily application]
│   ├── Brachot Rishonot
│   ├── Brachot Acharonot
│   ├── Tefillat Shacharit / Mincha / Maariv
│   └── Shabbat & Yom Tov Tefillah
├── Shabbat                     [Weight: High — weekly, complex]
│   ├── Melachot d'Oraita
│   ├── Melachot d'Rabbanan
│   ├── Muktzeh
│   └── Oneg & Kavod Shabbat
├── Kashrut                     [Weight: High — constant application]
│   ├── Basar b'Chalav
│   ├── Ta'aruvot
│   └── Bishul Akum / Pas Akum
├── Niddah & Taharat HaMishpacha [Weight: High — life-cycle]
├── Moadim                      [Weight: Medium-High — annual cycle]
├── Choshen Mishpat             [Weight: Medium — business & interpersonal]
└── ... and many more domains
```

> 💡 **The full map is one of the key contributions of this project** — if you have expertise in any halachic domain and want to help refine the weights and structure, we would love your input.
