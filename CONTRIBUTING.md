# Contributing to the Halachic Mastery System

Thank you for your interest. This project sits at the meeting point of Torah scholarship, pedagogy, and software — and it needs all three. Whatever your strength, there is a place for you here.

> **A guiding principle:** we are building something people will rely on to learn halacha. Accuracy, humility, and clarity come before speed. When in doubt, flag it and ask.

---

## Where to Start

**New here?** Open a [Discussion](https://github.com/ytkbaum/halachic-mastery/discussions) and introduce yourself — your background and what draws you to the project. We'll point you to where you can help most. Then:

- ⭐ **Star the repo** to follow progress
- 💬 **[Discussions](https://github.com/ytkbaum/halachic-mastery/discussions)** — ideas, questions, proposals, debate over weights and structure
- 🐛 **[Issues](https://github.com/ytkbaum/halachic-mastery/issues)** — gaps in the map, inaccuracies, feature requests, bugs
- 🔀 **Pull requests** — direct contributions to content or code

## Ways to Contribute

### 📜 Halachic Content & Mapping

The heart of the project. Help build and refine the weighted map of halachah:

- Propose or debate the weight of a topic on any of the seven axes (see `docs/METHODOLOGY.md`)
- Identify a topic's level of obligation (*d'oraysa / d'rabbanan / custom / ethical*) and severity
- Flag everyday derivatives — the common living form of a rare formal mitzvah
- Map prerequisite relationships between topics
- Review entries for accuracy

> **Important:** Halachic content must cite its sources. We do not invent severities, obligations, or rulings — where a source is silent, we leave the field open. Practical *pesak* is always deferred to a competent halachic authority.

## Working with the Map

The current weighted corpus lives as a live-formula workbook with every score, tier, rank, and level computed by formula — change a weight and the structure re-ranks. Before proposing a weight change:

1. Read `docs/METHODOLOGY.md` so we share the same definitions of the seven axes.
2. Open a Discussion or Issue describing the change and the reasoning — which axis, from what to what, and why.
3. Debate is welcome and expected. Weights are judgment calls; the value is in making the reasoning explicit.

---

## Standards

| Principle | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| **Sources over assertion** | Cite. Reword copyrighted translations rather than reproducing them; primary Hebrew texts in the public domain may be quoted. |
| **Proportion** | Keep *d'oraysa*, *d'rabbanan*, custom, and ethical distinct. Do not flatten them. |
| **Humility** | This is a study-prioritization aid, not a posek. Nothing here decides practical halacha. |
| **Respect** | Disagree about substance, never about people. This is a *beis midrash* in spirit. |

---

## Code of Conduct

Be respectful, honest, and constructive. Assume good faith. We are here to build something worthy of the subject.
